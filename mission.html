Our mission

Hands-on learning in a loving Christian environment

A Little Schoolhouse is located in beautiful Parrish, Florida. We offer a high quality preschool program by combining hands-on methods of education with a loving Christian environment. Your child will emerge from our program as a confident, independent thinker with a lifetime love of learning.

We invite you to find out more about our programs and visit our beautiful school for a tour.

Accreditation

Fully accredited

A Little Schoolhouse Christian Learning Academy is fully accredited by the Florida Association of Christian Colleges and Schools (FACCS) and the National Council for Private School Accreditation (NCPSA).

Open to All

A Little Schoolhouse is not affiliated with a particular church denomination. ALSCLA is open to all students regardless of race, religion, or national origin. Admission is at the discretion of the Director and each classroom is balanced to the best extent with regard to age and sex.

Code of Ethics

ALSCLA complies with The Code of Ethics and The Principles of Professional Conduct of The Education Profession in Florida as set forth by The Florida Department of Education, State Board of Education, Rule 6B-1.006, FAC.

Reporting Misconduct

Florida Department of Education requires that all private schools must post policies and procedures for reporting misconduct by instructional personnel or school administrators which affect the health, safety, or welfare of a student. All staff and faculty employed by ALSCLA have been instructed and trained in Ethics Code and on Reporting Misconduct and Child Abuse.

A Little Schoolhouse Christian Learning Academy is a child care facility operating under an exemption pursuant to the laws of the State of Florida and is not subject to licensure or regulation by the Department of Children and Families.
