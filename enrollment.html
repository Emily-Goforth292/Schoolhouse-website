Annual Program Fee

A non-refundable Annual Program Fee of $95 is collected at the time of registration for all preschool and VPK Plus families that gain enrollment.

If space is not available you will be given the option of being placed on our waiting list and will pay the Annual Program Fee upon placement. Spots are filled on a first come basis contingent upon availability.

VPK Enrollment

VPK enrollees must have their Florida VPK Certificate issued prior to enrollment. Certificates are issued by contacting the Manatee County Early Learning Coalition. The link is supplied below.

No Year-Long Contract

Little Schoolhouse does not require a year contract like many programs. We simply ask for a 30 day written advance notice if you must withdraw your child.
